GhostTrim privacy policy

Publisher: Mintleaf Studio. Last updated: October 24, 2026.

Summary

GhostTrim does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. Its own code makes no network requests. It has no account and no analytics.

What is stored, and where

Your saved prompts and settings are stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device and are removed when you uninstall the extension. Text you type in the editor is not stored unless you press Save.

Page access

Without optional site access, GhostTrim touches a page only when you click Insert, Tighten or Count, and only to place or read the text you chose. Token counting and Shorten run locally in the extension.

Optional site access: the first time you press Insert, Chrome asks whether GhostTrim may access these supported AI chat sites: chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, grok.com, perplexity.ai, www.perplexity.ai, copilot.microsoft.com, chat.deepseek.com, chat.mistral.ai, www.meta.ai and meta.ai. GhostTrim does not ask for access to all sites. Without access, or on other sites, Insert copies the text so you can paste it. With access, GhostTrim only places your text into the chat box you pick on those sites. To do that it adds a small listener on those pages that remembers which text box you last clicked, kept only in that page. It never reads what you type there and sends nothing anywhere. You can see and remove allowed sites in the side panel under "Sites allowed", or in chrome://extensions.

On-device AI

Shorten uses Chrome's built-in on-device model when available. GhostTrim sends nothing; Chrome itself downloads the model once. The model may change wording, so GhostTrim shows a before/after view with Undo; review before sending.
