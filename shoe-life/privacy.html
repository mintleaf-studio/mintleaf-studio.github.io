What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. Shoe Life is an on-device app. Pair names, step logs, check notes, optional weight band, optional photos, and settings are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB on this device only.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics SDKs
  • No third-party tracking or advertising
  • No server uploads
  • No network calls after install (except optional same-origin asset updates when you host the files yourself)

Export and wipe

You can export your data as a local JSON file, import a JSON file you choose, or wipe all data from Settings. Wipe deletes the IndexedDB database and clears this app’s caches on the device.

Optional notifications

If you allow notifications, they are local only (no push server). You can deny or revoke them in your browser or OS settings.

Health wording

Shoe Life estimates usage only. It can’t tell you how your shoes or your body are doing. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.
