What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. Shoe Life is an on-device app. Pair names, step logs, check notes, optional weight band, optional pair photos, and settings are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB on this device only.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics SDKs
  • No third-party tracking or advertising
  • No server uploads
  • No network calls after install (except optional same-origin asset updates when you host the files yourself, and links you choose to tap)

Pair photos

If you add a photo of a pair (camera or a file you pick), it is shrunk to about 512 pixels on your device and saved in IndexedDB on this device only. Photos are never uploaded. They are included in a JSON export you make yourself, and removed when you wipe data.

Links you tap

When a pair reaches its estimated check point, the app can show “Replace the cushioning” and “Shop new shoes” buttons. They open a web search (DuckDuckGo) in a new tab only when you tap them. Nothing about your pairs or steps is sent except the search words in that link (for example “replacement insoles” plus the brand and model you typed). No tracking parameters are added.

Affiliate disclosure

Right now Shoe Life uses no affiliate links. In a future version, some of these links may be affiliate links; we may earn a commission if you buy through them. When that happens, a short note will appear next to those links in the app, and this section will be updated.

Export and wipe

You can export your data as a local JSON file, import a JSON file you choose, or wipe all data from Settings. Wipe deletes the IndexedDB database and clears this app’s caches on the device.

Optional notifications

If you allow notifications, they are local only (no push server). You can deny or revoke them in your browser or OS settings.

Health wording

Shoe Life estimates usage only. It can’t tell you how your shoes or your body are doing. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.
