    Check your shoes

    A short look-and-press routine. Record what you see. This app cannot tell you how your shoes or your body are doing.

    1. Look (about 20 seconds)
    • Creasing in the midsole side walls
    • Outsole worn through to foam
    • Heel counter leaning
    2. Press-test the heel

    Press the heel foam with your thumb. Compare with how it felt when new, or with a new pair of the same model.

    Visual check guide (optional)

    Three quick checks. Your answers suggest a result in step 4; you can always change it.

    A. Crease test

    Look at the side of the foam under the heel and forefoot. Are there deep creases that stay when the shoe is not being worn?

    B. Flat-table tilt test

    Put the shoe on a flat table and look at it from behind at eye level. Does it lean to one side?

    C. Thumb-press rebound

    Press the heel foam firmly with your thumb, then let go. Compare with a newer pair if you have one. Does it feel flat or slow to spring back?

    3. Optional gauge or durometer

    Gauge bands are design targets, not validated. See GAUGE.md.
