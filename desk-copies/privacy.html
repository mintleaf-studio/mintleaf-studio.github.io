Privacy policy

Desk Copies · Mintleaf Studio

Effective: October 14, 2026

Desk Copies photographs pages, straightens them, and saves them on your phone.

What we collect

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of ours for your documents. The app does not request an internet permission. Pages, photos, and any text read from a page stay on this device.

Camera

The camera is used only when you take a picture. That picture is stored in the app’s private storage on the device.

Photos you import

Import uses the system photo picker. You choose the pictures. The app does not ask for permission to read your whole photo library, and it does not request a storage permission.

Text recognition

With Desk Copies Pro, text is read on this device by a model included in the app. That text is saved with the document on this device. It is not sent to a server.

Purchases

Desk Copies Pro is a one-time purchase. Tips are optional and do not unlock features. There is no subscription. Google Play processes the payment. The app does not see or store your card number. The app asks Google Play, on this device, whether Pro has been bought so the purchase can be restored.

Password

If you export a password-protected PDF, the password is used only to encrypt that file. It is not saved.

Documents

Pages you save stay on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Android backup for this app is turned off, so the app does not hand those files to a cloud backup.

Share

When you share a PDF or JPEG, Android gives that file to the app you pick. What happens next is up to that app and its own policy. Desk Copies does not upload the file.

Permissions

The only permission you are asked for is the camera. There is no internet permission, and the app does not ask to read your files. The install includes the Play billing permission so a purchase can be completed, and a signature permission added by the Android libraries so other apps cannot call this app’s private components. Those are not prompts, and they do not allow the app to open a network connection or read your files.

Children

Desk Copies is a general tool. It is not directed at children, and it does not collect personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. The same text is available inside the app, which does not load this page from the network.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email Mintleaf Studio at morales07003@gmail.com. The app has no account system and does not collect an email address.
