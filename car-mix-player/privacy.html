Privacy policy

Car Mix Player · Mintleaf Studio

Effective: October 2026

Car Mix Player plays audio files that are already stored on your phone.

Data collected

No data is collected unless cover lookup is on. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

Internet

The app can look up a missing album cover, and only if you turn on Look up missing covers in Settings. That switch is off until you choose it. Wi-Fi only is on by default. A lookup runs on Wi-Fi or Ethernet, and it is skipped while the phone is offline or on mobile data with that option on. You can turn Wi-Fi only off in Settings. While lookup is on, the artist name and the album name are sent to MusicBrainz (MetaBrainz Foundation) to find a release, then one front cover is downloaded from the Cover Art Archive. Cover Art Archive images are served from archive.org by the Internet Archive. Like any web request, the phone's IP address and the app's User-Agent are sent and may be logged by those services. The song file, your listening history, and anything else on the phone are not sent. Requests are limited to about one per second. Covers are saved on the phone. Only a real no-match is remembered. A timeout, an offline phone, or a server error is not stored as a permanent miss. Turning the switch off stops new requests and clears lookups that are still waiting. The internet permission exists only for this lookup. While cover lookup is on, a car, watch, or other media controller that asks for artwork can start one of those lookups in the background. The browse list itself does not wait for the network.

What stays on the phone

The app reads your on-device music library, including embedded tags and pictures, so it can play those files. The on-device scan stores a tempo, mel-band levels, and a musical-key estimate for each song. Those estimates stay on the phone. Listening counts, favorites, a resume position for long tracks, appearance choices, and playback settings are stored in the app's private storage. A new phone can receive modes, corrections, favorites, play history, and settings through Android backup, or through a backup file you export in Settings. That export stays on your devices. The app does not upload it. Uninstalling the app removes that private storage.

Android backup

Android backup is turned on. It can include this app's private library data in your Google account backup.

Car connection

Resumes when Android Auto connects, or on car Bluetooth on supported phones. If you turn that on, Bluetooth permission is used only to notice a car stereo. The app does not store device addresses, contacts, or a list of nearby devices. A connected car media session can browse the on-device library while that session is open. That exchange stays between the phone and the car.

Crash reports

If the app stops unexpectedly, the next launch can show the last crash report saved in the app's private storage. Share opens the system share sheet with that text. Nothing is sent until you share it.

Sharing

No data is sold. When cover lookup is on, the artist name, the album name, the phone's IP address, and the app's User-Agent go to MusicBrainz (MetaBrainz Foundation) and the Internet Archive. No data is sent to the developer unless you choose to share a crash report.
